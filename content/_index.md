# About Me

Hi! I am a graduate student pursuing a PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **Duke University**, advised by Professor Xiang Cheng.

My research interests include diffusion/flow-matching models for image generation, continuous diffusion language models, and representation learning.

Prior to grad school, I completed my B.S.E. at Duke, double-majoring in Electrical and Computer Engineering and Computer Science with a minor in German. As an undergraduate, I worked with Professors Tingjun Chen, Neil Gong, and Emily Wenger on various flavours of machine learning research.
